# Request your workspace

Your workspace is your own isolated space to run agents in: an identity, a storage area and a namespace that only your work uses.

## Request it

1. Open [the setup checklist](/setup).
2. At **Request your workspace**, press **Request my workspace**.
   You should see "Workspace requested — waiting for an admin to approve".
3. Wait for an admin. When they approve, the step shows "Setting up your workspace", then "Workspace ready" a few minutes later.

From Claude Code you can do the same: type `/sc:setup` and follow its first step.

## What the admin does

A platform admin approves the request in one of two places:

1. On [Home](/), in the card **Needs you**: press **Approve** (or **Deny…** with a reason).
2. Or on [Admin › Platform people](/admin/people), in the card **Waiting for you**: press **Approve** or **Deny**.

They should see "Approved … The job is starting."

## If it was not approved

* The step shows "Your workspace request was not approved" and the admin's reason.
* Press **Request again** after 24 hours, or ask the admin first.

## Join a team's workspace instead

A workspace admin can share a join link (`/join#…`).

1. Open the link. You should see the page **Join a workspace**.
2. Add **A note for its admins (optional)** and press **Ask to join**.
3. The request shows under **Your requests to join**. An admin approves it on [Admin › Platform people](/admin/people).
4. Changed your mind? Press **Withdraw**.
