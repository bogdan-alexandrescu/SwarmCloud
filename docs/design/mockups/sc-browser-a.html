SwarmCloud pane: proposal A, Tree

One of three competing proposals (A Tree, B Two columns, C Table and drawer) for following SwarmCloud work from Claude Code: a SwarmCloud pane beside the chat, drawn by the sc plugin, plus one /workflows row per workflow. BGD picks per frame on the picks page. The others: B Two columns, C Table and drawer.

Idea

One list that opens in place, like a file tree: a workflow opens to its stages under it, a stage to its agents under that, an agent to its details under that. → opens, ← closes, at every level.

Pros
  • One gesture at every level, and nothing moves sideways: what you opened stays on screen above what you open next.
  • Two workflows, or two stages, can be open at once and compared.
  • The simplest pane to build: one list of rows, each with an indent.
Risks
  • A 13-step stage with one agent open pushes the other workflows below the fold; Level 4 draws only the four agents around the open one.
  • Each level's indent costs columns: at 80 the tree indents by 2 and 4 instead of 5 and 9.
  • At 60 columns the details wrap onto many lines.
What every frame obeys
  • Stages are the console's, by name and count. A step's stage is its dependency level plus one, the console's levelsOf (apps/swarm-ui/src/dag.ts), named Stage N as the workflow page labels each band. The shape line is the console's page head, 15 steps in 3 stages (1 → 13 → 1), and a stage's census is the band's own (stageCensus: failures first, then the biggest count first). /sc:swarmcloud already numbers its step rows by this rule (plugin/workflows/run.js), so the pane and the rows agree.
  • Nothing is read until somebody asks. The pane reads when it opens, when something is opened, and on Refresh. It has no timer. Each read is one sc MCP tool call made by the pane over the engine's connection, so no pane click costs model tokens.
  • Only the /workflows rows run while nobody browses, one per running workflow, to notice a finish.
  • Honesty: an unknown figure is a dash with its reason, never 0. A parked step holds no capacity and is drawn as waiting, never as running (CONTRACT invariant 1).
  • Drawn at 144 and 80 columns (the narrow frame at 60). The terminal follows the page theme: switch Light and Dark at the top. A selected row has a tinted background, and controls are drawn as [Refresh], with Selects as [… ▾].
  • All data is example data, read at 10:42:07Z; the host is swarm.example.com. Every total is the sum of the rows drawn (4 in this session: 11.5M tokens, $38.34).
