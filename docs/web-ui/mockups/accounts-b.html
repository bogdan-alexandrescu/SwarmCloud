What every frame obeys
  • PICKS.md: nothing below 12px, no all-caps, no letter-spacing, a light / dark / system toggle in the panel's user foot, and honesty: an unknown figure is a dash with its reason, never 0.
  • The V2 Sky spine (navigation.html) and the console's own tokens (styles.css). Capacity's pages are the ones App.tsx SECTIONS has today: Pools, Runtimes, By runner profile, Holders, Accounts, Provider quota, API reads.
  • The broker's rule as built (quota_broker/accounts.py after PR 2223 and PR 2231): a 15% floor on the 5-hour window and 5% on the 7-day window, an aged reading (older than 30 minutes) counted at half, at most max_agents agents per account (6 by default), and GET /v1/accounts' can_take_new_agent, blocked_by and next_eligible_at.
  • Data. Frames B1 and B2 draw #2222's table as read on 2026-10-24 (accounts at 09:04Z, the 28 waiting at 09:16Z). Frame B3's hold counts and blocks are #2222's 14:04Z measurement. Everything else that is not in #2222 (agent ids, hold deadlines, 5-hour figures after 09:04Z, loans other than team's and saga-team's, the forecast's pace) is marked example in the frame or its caption.

Idea

The page is a time chart. Every account is a lane on one shared axis, from now to seven days out. Each lane draws its two windows as level bands, with the floor as a dashed line, so "under its floor" is a band below a line, coloured. The instant the band rises back over the line is a marker with its clock time. A vertical next free slot line runs through every lane at the first instant any account can take an agent, and the waiting agents queue against it. Above the lanes, one pool row counts the accounts that can take an agent over the week, so when the pool goes dark and comes back is drawn, not inferred. Opening an account expands its lane in place: taller bands, its agents as bars running to their hold deadlines, and its settings underneath.

Pros

  • It answers "when" as well as "whether". On 2026-10-24 the question was not "is anything free" but "when does anything free", and the next-slot line and the pool row say 10:10Z without arithmetic.
  • Reset windows cannot read as their old figure. Past its reset a window is drawn full, with the reset time written on it, so a band never shows 40% for a window that rolled over hours ago.
  • The floor is a drawn line on every lane, so the 15% and 5% floors are visible where they decide things, not in a help page.
  • Duplicates line up: two lanes with identical bands and markers one second apart are visible at a glance, before the flag says so.

Risks

  • A 5-hour window is invisible on a 7-day axis. The axis is split: the first quarter is the next 12 hours, the rest is the week. A split axis is easy to misread; the break is drawn and both zones are labelled.
  • Band height is the remaining share on a square-root scale, so a 5% floor is not flattened to one pixel. A square-root scale is not intuitive; every band also writes its figure in words.
  • A full account frees when an agent finishes, which has no clock. The lane shows its hold deadlines as the latest instant, which can read as a prediction. It is labelled "latest", never "expected".
  • The forecast needs a history of readings. The broker keeps only the latest reading per window today, so the pace row needs new storage before it can be built (decided-and-open, below).
  • Seven lanes of chart is denser than a table on a phone; the phone stacks each lane's words above its track and drops the inline reset labels to the words.

B1The pool as read at 09:04Z on 2026-10-24, under the old floor

#2222's table drawn as the old rule judged it: one 15% floor on both windows, and no per-account cap. Every lane is under a floor. The next-slot line sits at 10:10Z, when aieng-claude1's 5-hour window resets, and the 28 waiting agents queue against it, all of them bound for that one account. The pool row says the same thing for the week: dark until 10:10Z, then one account until Sun 25 18:59Z.

B2The same pool under the new floors, with the forecast

The same 09:04Z reading under today's rule: 15% on the 5-hour window, 5% on the 7-day, and 6 agents per account. The dashed floor line drops on every 7-day band, and three bands now sit over it: ai-eng-2, devops-main and devops-team. Their 18 slots take 18 of the 28 at once; the next-slot line moves to 10:10Z for the other 10. A second pool row draws the forecast at the last day's pace.

B3Full accounts: the pool at 14:04Z, ten minutes after the cap was deployed

A full account has no clock: it frees when one of its agents finishes. Its lane says "Full · 6 of 6" in words, and draws its agents as bars running to their hold deadlines, the latest any of them can still hold a slot. The pool row counts only the slots a clock frees.

B4devops-team: the lane expanded

Opening an account expands its lane in place and folds the others to one line each, so the shared axis and the next-slot line stay in view. The expanded lane draws its windows tall, a third track of its agents, and underneath them the windows in words, its agents, and the account's settings and actions as the built page has them.

B5A duplicate subscription: team and saga-personal

On 2026-10-24 eng:team turned out to be saga-personal registered a second time: one claude.ai login holds two organisations, Saga (Team) and Personal (Max), and the sign-in authorised the one selected. The two lanes sit side by side under one bracket, their bands identical and their reset markers a minute apart. The expanded lane says what to do.

B6No account registered

The axis is still drawn, with nothing on it, so the page reads as the same page with no lanes rather than as an error. The answer names what that means for agents that need a pool credential.
