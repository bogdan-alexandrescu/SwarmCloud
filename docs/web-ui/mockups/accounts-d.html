Capacity › Accounts: proposal D, Account cards and forecast

One of four competing proposals (A Answer first, B Timeline, C Window grid, D Account cards and forecast) for the changed Capacity › Accounts page. BGD picks one. Every frame is drawn at desktop width (1440, or this page's width when the window is narrower, with the rest in a sideways scroller) and at 390 wide. The theme control at the top, and the one in every desktop frame's panel foot, repaints all of them light, dark or as the system says.

What every frame keeps
  • PICKS.md: nothing below 12px, no all-caps, no letter-spacing, a light / dark / system toggle in the panel's user foot, and honesty: an unknown figure is a dash with its reason, never 0.
  • The V2 Sky spine (navigation.html) and the product as built: Overview · Work · Automate · Capacity · Admin, with Help and the avatar at the foot (App.tsx SECTIONS, Spine.tsx). Capacity's panel reads Pools, Runtimes, Holders, Accounts (Subscription accounts, Provider quota) and API reads.
  • The broker's rule as built (quota_broker/accounts.py, PR 2223 and PR 2231): a 15% floor on the 5-hour window and on any window it does not name, a 5% floor on the 7-day window, an aged reading (over 30 min) counted at half, and max_agents per account (default 6). Each card is drawn from GET /v1/accounts' can_take_new_agent, blocked_by, next_eligible_at, max_agents and windows.
  • The figures in D1 to D4 and D7 are #2222's table, read 2026-10-24 09:04Z (waiting agents read 09:16Z). Everything else is example, and the frame says so where it is drawn.

Idea

A strip across the top answers for the pool: how many accounts can take a new agent now, which frees first and when, and how many agents wait (a link to them). Under it, in the same strip, a forecast: the pool's weekly burn rate, when the pool goes dark at this pace and when it comes back, a seven-day ribbon of dark and open hours, and how much of the week laptop sessions spent. Below the strip, one card per account: a state chip in words, its organisation and plan, a gauge per window with its floor drawn on it, the sentence that says what blocks it and when that clears, then holds n of 6 and its loans. A card opens the account's detail.

Pros

  • The question the page failed on 2026-10-24 ("can the pool take an agent?") is answered in the first line, not summed from rows.
  • The floor is on the gauge: a bar that stops short of its tick is visibly blocked, without reading a percentage.
  • The forecast turns a weekly shortfall into a date. On the 09:04Z readings the pool spends about 11 account-weeks a week against 6 subscriptions, which no per-window figure says.
  • The forecast needs no new history: each account's pace is its used share over the hours since its window opened (resets_at minus 7 days), all in today's reading.
  • Cards hold a wrong sign-in in plain sight: the organisation and plan line, and the duplicate flag, sit on the card.

Risks

  • Cards are taller than rows: 7 accounts take three rows at 1440, and a pool of 20 means scrolling to compare. Sorting (can take first, then soonest to clear) softens it.
  • The forecast is a model. Average pace since the window opened hides a burst, and the pool's pace is assumed to move onto whichever account is open. It is labelled "at this pace", and its method is in this page's Open list, but a reader may take it as a promise.
  • "Laptop sessions spent" cannot be measured yet. The broker keeps only the latest reading and sees totals, not who spent them. It is drawn as a dash with that reason in every today frame, and only D5 shows it filled, as an example.
  • Organisation and plan come from POOL-DUP-DETECT, which has not shipped. Until it does, every account but a flagged duplicate reads "not recorded yet".

Decided, and open

Decided (and drawn as decided)

  • Floors per window: 5-hour 15%, 7-day 5%, any other window 15% (BGD, 2026-10-24 09:16Z; PR 2223).
  • An aged reading, over 30 min old, counts at half. It is drawn as a pale bar to the share left and a solid bar to the share counted, with "counted as". Hatching stays reserved for "not measured" (Account.blocking_windows).
  • max_agents per account, default 6, owner-settable 1 to 50 (PR 2231). "Full" frees when an agent finishes, and next_eligible_at is null for it, so the card says no clock knows.
  • A never-measured account takes one agent until its first reading (BGD, 10:57Z; in the POOL-DUP-DETECT brief).
  • A pool account's identity is the organisation, not the login (09:43Z). Each card shows "organisation · plan".
  • Duplicate subscriptions are flagged and only the older registration is handed out (POOL-DUP-DETECT).
  • A window past its reset reads as reset, never as its old figure with ~ (#2222).

Open, for BGD

  • The forecast's method. Pace = used ÷ hours since the window opened, summed over distinct subscriptions; the pool's pace moves to whichever account is open; the 5-hour windows are not forecast. Is "at this pace" enough of a label, or should the ribbon show a range?
  • Laptop sessions' share. It needs the broker to keep reading history and to compare window movement with the account's holds (use while it held no agent is someone else's). Nothing keeps that history today. Build it, or keep the dash?
  • Organisation and plan depend on POOL-DUP-DETECT recording the identity at add and refresh. Until then the line reads "not recorded yet" on every account.
  • Which duplicate is handed out is POOL-DUP-DETECT's rule (the older registration). These frames do not claim which of team and saga-personal is older.
  • Sort order. Drawn as: can take first, then soonest to clear. Alternative: a fixed order by label, so a card does not move between reads.
  • Phone forecast. Drawn in full under the answer. It could fold to one line ("dark 21:26Z, back Oct 25 18:59Z") with the ribbon behind a tap.
