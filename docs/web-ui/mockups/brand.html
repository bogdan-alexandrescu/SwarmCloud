Idea

Today's hexagon mark, kept and finished: the six vertices become filled agents, the ring fades, and three spokes tie them to a core. Below 24px it switches to a compact cut (seven larger dots, no ring or spokes), the same idea as today's COMPACT geometry. The owner chose this mark on 2026-10-01.

Pros

  • Continuity: anyone who has used the console recognises it.
  • Draws only in the gradient, so it sits on any ground without a tile.

Risks

  • Twelve parts in the full cut, so two cuts must be maintained and the switch at 24px has to be applied everywhere the mark is drawn.
  • A hexagon of nodes is a common "network" glyph; the gradient and the faded ring are what make it ours.

3Task states

Every state is drawn as a mark and a word. The mark's shape names the state on its own, so the console still reads on a phone (where the word is hidden), in greyscale, and for the roughly 8% of men who cannot separate amber from red. Hue says which family a state belongs to: teal-green for the four states that hold capacity, violet for parked, red for failed, and grey for everything that costs nothing or has ended.

What changes from today

  • QUEUED, READY and PARKED get three marks (ring, ring with dot, pause bars) instead of one shared triangle. The triangle goes back to meaning "warning".
  • LEASED, DISPATCHED and STARTING share a half-filled disc: they hold a slot but are not working yet. RUNNING alone gets the haloed disc. This makes invariant 3 (concurrency counts from LEASED) visible: every green mark is a slot in use.
  • The live hue moves from the accent blue to teal-green, so the brand colour is never mistaken for a state.
  • CANCELLED keeps the grey flat bar from the #129 ruling. DEAD_LETTERED gets its own outlined diamond, so it is told apart from a plain failure.

5Notes on the decision

Palette A (Open sky), mark M1 (Hive), Inter Tight with DM Mono

Owner's picks, 2026-10-01

  • Open sky keeps AI Studio's structure exactly (accent, accent-2, soft tint, deep-navy dark mode, sky-to-deep-blue mark gradient). Every text pair passes AA in both themes with room to spare; the weakest is mute on bg in light at 6.20:1.
  • accent-2 is for the mark and the focus ring only. In light it is 3.30:1 on white: enough for a graphic (3:1), not for text. Text in the accent always uses --accent.
  • Hive keeps the console's existing hexagon and finishes it. It has two cuts: the full mark from 24px up (lockups, the spine, app icons), and a compact cut of seven dots below 24px (favicon, browser tab, phone header). It draws without a tile, in one of three gradients: light-ground, dark-ground, and the bright one (white → #7dd3fc) used on the spine and the phone header (§2b).
  • States: eleven states, nine silhouettes, four hues. The shapes alone separate running, parked and failed, as the greyscale strip shows. Open question: #405 (owner, 2026-09-30) tints workflow nodes light blue for the four live states, green for succeeded and cream for waiting. Those hues disagree with this page's (teal-green live, grey succeeded, violet parked). See workflows.html.
  • Fonts: no measured reason to change from AI Studio's pair. In the app they should be self-hosted (bundled with the UI), so the console makes no third-party request.

6What exists

The frame today, from Brand.tsx

  • Mark: SwarmMark, a hexagon of six vertex dots around a core, stroked in currentColor at 36px. A COMPACT cut exists for small sizes and the favicon.
  • Wordmark: <b>Swarm</b>Cloud in the system sans. No webfont is loaded anywhere; --font is ui-sans-serif, -apple-system, ….
  • Environment badge: measured, never assumed. The API's declared environment wins over the build's, which wins over a loopback host. Production gets a full-width bar.
  • Identity: tenant name with a copy-id button, "signed in" email, and an admin badge, all in the header row.

Colour and state today, from design-system.md §1 and §6.6

  • One accent, --info (#0969da light, #58a6ff dark). It is the link hover, the focus ring and the live mark. Selection deliberately has no hue.
  • Five state hues (ok, warn, bad, info, paused) and seven mark silhouettes. Healthy is grey, by the CH-17 ruling.
  • The gap: stateTone maps QUEUED, READY and PARKED all to wait, which draws one triangle. A parked agent and a queued one look the same in the Agents list, and the warning triangle is spent on states that are not warnings.

What carries over: the measured environment badge, grey-for-healthy, every state as a shape plus a word, and the "one accent" budget. What changes: the accent moves from link blue to sky blue and becomes the brand colour, the active row gets a tint and a left tick (the AI Studio method), the type becomes Inter Tight with DM Mono, and the waiting states get three different marks.

Idea

A cloud whose lower edge breaks into a row of agents, with two drifting off to the right. Says the name literally: a swarm, in a cloud.

Pros

  • Reads as "cloud" at any size, so the product needs no tagline.
  • Friendly, and distinct from the hexagon.

Risks

  • Literal marks age fastest, and cloud glyphs are everywhere (status pages, storage products, weather).
  • The drifting dots vanish below 24px.

Idea

Three darts flying in a V on a sky tile: one lead and two followers. A swarm with a direction, which is what a scheduler does with agents.

Pros

  • A solid tile with three white shapes holds up at 16px and as a favicon.
  • Shares the AI Studio method (gradient tile, white figure) without sharing its look.
  • The lead dart doubles as a "go" glyph for the Submit action.

Risks

  • Arrows read as "play" or "forward"; next to a media control it could be mistaken for one.
  • Needs the tile; on its own the darts are thin.
