Account health: pick a design

Four complete proposals for showing each subscription account’s health: its rate-limit hits, errors, refusals and re-auths, from the record ACCOUNT-429-SCOPE serves. Each draws the same five frames from the same example record, so they can be compared side by side. Pick one design whole, or one proposal per frame, then press Copy my picks and paste the text into the PR or the issue.

The previews are the proposals’ own frames, scaled down to show their shape. To read one, open it at full size; it opens the proposal at that frame. Every figure is an example: one invented pool (tenant eng, 7 accounts) and one invented 7-day record.

F3Pool-wide over time

Rate-limit hits and errors over time across accounts: which account hit which window, when.

A table of days

One row per account, one cell per day. Each hit is named by window and time, and the rest are counted.

A swimlane of events

The pool per hour over the top, then a lane per account with a mark per event and a band per limited stretch.

C

A heat map of hours

The strip at 7 days: three thin rows per account, a cell per hour, stronger for more, with each hit named.

D

Incident bars

A bar per incident in each account’s lane, solid while open, then the closed incidents as a table.
