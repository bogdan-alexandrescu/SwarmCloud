One small chart per measure, every chart on the same time axis, stacked top to bottom: a grid of sparklines grown up into charts with scales. Pointing at one bucket picks it in every chart, and a breakdown recolours every chart at once.

BSmall multiples

Idea

Seven measures, seven charts, one time axis. Each row is a chart with its own scale (0 to the largest bucket it measured, never rounded up), and its left column holds the measure's name, its unit, the total over the span and that total's coverage. The rows line up bucket for bucket, so a week that was busy, slow, token-heavy or expensive reads straight down the page. Agents and their retries share one chart: agents above the line, retries below it on the same scale. Pointing at a bucket (or tapping it on a phone) lights that bucket in all seven rows and turns every left column into that bucket's figures. Picking a breakdown stacks every bar by the same groups in the same colours, with one legend for the grid.

Pros

  • No dual axis anywhere: hours, tokens and dollars never share a scale, so no unit has to be squeezed or indexed.
  • Each measure carries its own coverage beside its own figure ("6,720 of 6,755 attempts reported", F2), so a gap in tokens never reads as a gap in agents.
  • The shared axis answers "what else happened that week" without a second screen.
  • It reflows to a phone by stacking the left column above each chart; no chart scrolls sideways and the page never does.
  • Uses only bars on one baseline, the mark the Timeline's outcome ledger already draws, and the existing hatch for "not measured".

Risks

  • A breakdown stacks. The chart layer has no stack primitive and charts/README.md says "nothing here stacks", because a stack gives an absent segment height 0. This proposal needs a stack that draws only measured segments and puts an "at least" mark on the bucket instead (F5). That is a new rule for the build to hold and test.
  • Seven rows is a tall grid: on a laptop the top rows scroll away from the one time axis at the bottom. The build would keep that axis sticky.
  • Small rows flatten small groups: a group under about 3% of a bucket is a 1-2px sliver. The readout and the legend's totals carry those numbers.
  • The five series colours are not separable in greyscale and series 4 and 5 are close even in colour (the dataviz validator puts that pair at ΔE 13.6, under its 15 floor). Every breakdown has a legend, groups past the fourth fold into "Other" in series 5, and the readout names every group in words.
  • Picking a breakdown is a new read of the same days, so it is priced and waits for Run (F6); the recolour is instant only after that read.

The reflow rules
  • The page column is 390. Every chart is drawn at 342 (390 less 12px of page and 12px of card padding on each side), its own drawing with its own tick count, not the desktop chart scaled down; so tick text stays 12px.
  • The left column moves above its chart: name and unit, the figure, the coverage. The chart keeps all 26 weeks; a bar is about 7px wide with a surface gap between buckets.
  • The value scale keeps two labels, 0 and the largest bucket, in a 44px gutter. Units shorten to "M", "h" and "$" there; the row heading spells them out.
  • The axis labels every sixth week (every second month), once, under the last chart. "so far", "not read" and a month's year go on its second line.
  • Buckets stay a three-way segment; the breakdown becomes a picker button and a bottom sheet (F6d).
  • The live counts cards stack; their states go to two columns.
  • A tap, not a hover, picks a bucket; tapping it again clears it.

·For the build, if B is picked

What this proposal needs that the console does not have yet.

  • A stacked bar that cannot shorten. charts/README.md's rule stands: an absent segment is never given a height. The stack draws measured segments only, and a bucket with any absent or partial part carries the "at least" triangle and a "≥" in the readout. A test reads that off the SVG.
  • Two drawings per chart, wide and 300-ish, as every chart in the console is drawn; this page draws 800 and 342.
  • Every chart root is role="group", with one HTML column per bucket named with its time and every figure, one tab stop and a roving tabindex, as the outcome ledger does.
  • Run is the only reader. Buckets and breakdown re-price it and never read. The page never refreshes itself. Nor does anything else read these counts on a timer: the console shell's 30-second GET /v1/stats poll (Spine.tsx) carries the 24 platform counts for an admin today, and the build drops that half from the poll (#2149).
  • No change to the frozen contract: the v9 usage block already carries every field drawn here.
