Plans, proposal C: Pipeline board

One of four competing proposals (A to D) for how the console shows what automatic runs propose, and how a plan is judged and approved. Each draws the same frames (P1 to P7). This one draws the whole road from an automatic finding to a merged fix as one board of stages, so the reader sees every proposal at once and sees where the flow stalls.

Idea

Automate gains one tab, Pipeline, between Schedules and Approvals. It is a board of five stages: found (a failure fingerprint, an issue the sweep picked, an observer's proposal), planned, waiting on a person, approved and running, pull request and CI, and merged or ended. Every card says where it came from. A card that waits on a person is edged amber and sorts first in its column, oldest first, with its time to expiry. A plan opens in a drawer over the board (Subscription accounts A's side sheet) and is decided there: Approve, Edit, Reject with a reason, each carrying the digest of the plan shown. After approval the same card moves right, and its drawer becomes the trace from plan to merge.

How it differs from the console today

  • Today an issue run's plan is in Work › Runs, a proposal or a merge in Automate › Approvals, and the plan also as a GitHub comment. Here all of them are cards on one board; Approvals keeps the kinds that are not part of this road (run, spec, input).
  • Today the PLANNED view lists the plan's fields in the order they are stored. Here the drawer reads in the order a decision is made: why it exists, what it changes, what it collides with, what it costs, what risk it touches, then the buttons.
  • Today a column of state words is all that says where work stops. Here each column's head names its count and its oldest card, and a column holding something over a day is edged.
  • Today the source of a plan is a schedule id on the run. Here every card names its source in words: the sweep, failure review with its fingerprint, an observer, or a person.

Pros and risks

  • Pro: one look shows the flow and its stall: five waiting plans with nothing running reads differently from five running and one waiting.
  • Pro: a card keeps its place in the flow after the decision, so "what did my approval become" is the same card, one column right.
  • Risk: five columns at 1440 with the nav open leave about 200px a card. Titles wrap to three lines; the drawer carries the rest.
  • Risk: a board invites dragging. Nothing here drags: a card moves only when the run moves, and a decision is made only in the drawer, against a digest.
  • Risk: on a phone the board becomes one stage at a time, so the whole-flow view is a row of stage chips with counts rather than columns.
What every frame obeys
  • PICKS.md: nothing below 12px, no all-caps, no letter-spacing, a light / dark / system toggle (top of this page and in every frame's panel foot), an unknown figure is a dash with its reason, never 0. State words are lower case (waiting, approved, rejected, expired, superseded), never the API's enums.
  • The V2 Sky spine, Config E's tokens, lead strip, cards, chips and buttons, Subscription accounts A's side sheet, Automate A's landing and B's one-approval decision. The board's columns are the only new layout.
  • The canonical plan is the real run_fba2dc8f6ec9494dae2f on bogdan-alexandrescu/SwarmCloud#2583, quoted from its plan comment. Its schedule's gate approved it automatically at 00:06:03Z; P2 draws it as it would wait if that gate said plan: approve. Every other card is example data. The board is read at Wednesday 2026-10-28 03:00Z; the viewer is Operator, the platform owner, unless a frame says otherwise.

P1

Where proposals land: the Pipeline board

Automate › Pipeline. Thirteen cards from every source, in every state. The lead names what waits on a person across the whole board; the columns show where it waits.

P2

One plan to decide

The card for #2583 opens its plan in a drawer over the board. The drawer reads top to bottom in the order a decision is made, and its foot holds the decision.

P3

Editing a plan

Edit turns the drawer into the plan's editor, in place, against the digest it replaces. Every change is marked until it is saved; saving makes a new revision with a new digest, and only then can it be approved.

P4

After approval: the plan traced to what it became

An approved card moves right. Its drawer becomes the trace: the plan revision approved, each step's task, the pull request, CI and its fix rounds, the review's verdict on every requirement, the merge, and the keyword it earned.

P5

The automatic sources and their gates

The board's "Sources" button. Every schedule and observer that puts cards on the board, its gate as served (gate.run, gate.plan, gate.merge, gate.approvers), today's spend, and what it proposed and what came of it in seven days. Spend and counts are example data.

P6

Empty and honest states

The states.html tiers: a whole-section state takes the content area with the nav usable; a region state shows in place, in its column.

P7

Phone at 390

The board becomes one stage at a time: a row of stage chips with counts, the waiting stage first. A plan opens as a page, its decision in a sticky foot.

New fields this proposal needs
