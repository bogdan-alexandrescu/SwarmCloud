CWindow grid

Idea

One row per account, read across: who it is, its 5-hour bar, its 7-day bar, its agents against the cap. Every bar carries the line it must not cross, the floor for a window and the cap for agents, so “blocked” is something you see, not a word you trust. Rows sort by what frees first, the same in both panels, and the verdict above counts the same rows.

Pros

  • The defect in #2222 was a floor nobody could see. Here every floor is drawn on every bar, and moving it (C1 to C2) moves one line.
  • Which window binds is visible at a glance: six red ends in the 7-day panel and one in the 5-hour panel tell #2222’s story without reading a number.
  • The same shape carries the agent cap, so full is one more line, not a new concept.
  • A reset window is an empty bar with its reset instant, never an old figure with ~.
  • Totals agree by construction: the verdict counts the rows the panels draw.

Risks

  • Bars invite comparing heights, and a 5-hour 60% is not a 7-day 60%. The panels are apart and each names its floor to keep them from being read as one scale.
  • A wide grid: on a phone the two panels stack and an account’s two bars are a scroll apart. The third panel, “Each account”, carries the words for that reason.
  • Sorting by what frees first moves rows as clocks pass. A row that jumps under the pointer is a cost; the sort is held while the pointer is over the grid.
  • The forecast depends on a pace the console has to derive from successive readings; nothing serves it yet (open, below).
  • The “Signed in to” line needs POOL-DUP-DETECT’s organisation and plan; until that ships it can only read “organisation not read yet”, and the duplicate strip cannot appear.

·Decided and open

Decided in this proposal

  • The verdict counts the rows; the rows are sorted can-take first (most room first, choose()’s order), then an aged reading (re-read within 5 min), then full, then by next_eligible_at.
  • Both panels keep the same row order, so an account is one line across the page.
  • A floor is a line on the bar in used terms (85% and 95%), named in the panel head in left terms (15% and 5%), as BGD set them.
  • A reset window draws empty with its reset instant; a stale reading draws pale with its age; an unknown window draws under its own name.
  • The row’s detail opens in place under its bars, at the built address ?account=eng%3A<label>; on a phone it is its own screen.
  • The duplicate is a strip above the grid and a box in the detail, with Replace the credential first.

Open, for BGD

  • Which proposal: A, B, C or D.
  • Organisation and plan are not served yet: this proposal needs POOL-DUP-DETECT’s organisation and plan on GET /v1/accounts.
  • The forecast’s pace (how many hours of readings, per account or per pool) is the console’s own arithmetic and has no field; should the broker serve it instead?
  • Whether the Agents column belongs in the grid, or the cap is said in words only (two panels, strictly).
  • Whether the waiting link opens Work › Agents filtered, or a list on this page.

This is a proposal: nothing is built until BGD picks one of the four.
