BA page of its own

Idea

Accounts answers “who can take work”. Account health answers “what has been going wrong, where, and when”. The two questions get two pages. Capacity › Account health (/capacity/account-health) leads with four tiles, one per status word, and a verdict tile that links back to Accounts. Under them are the accounts worst first, with their last 24 hours by family, then a 7-day timeline: a lane per account, a mark per event, a band per limited stretch. Each account’s own page (/capacity/account-health/<id>) has room for the full counts, a wide hourly chart and an event log as a table.

Pros

  • Room. The event log is a real table, and the chart is 640 px wide rather than a sheet’s 440.
  • The timeline answers “which account hit which window when” at a glance: bands line up across lanes, so a provider-wide 529 afternoon (Oct 22) reads as one column.
  • A page someone can keep open during an incident. Accounts stays exactly as accounts-a builds it.

Risks

  • One more tab under Capacity (eight). An operator on Accounts has to know the other page exists. Each row on Accounts would link here.
  • The page makes one health read per account, every 60 s, while it is open: 7 here, more on a bigger pool.
  • The verdict tile repeats the Accounts answer in one line. It is drawn small and links there, but it is the same fact on two pages.

·Decided and open

Decided in this proposal

  • A new Capacity tab, Account health, after Accounts, at /capacity/account-health, with a page per account under it.
  • Status is the broker’s word, with icon and word, counted in tiles. The verdict tile is a one-line pointer to Accounts, not a second answer.
  • The timeline draws events, not a rate: one mark per event, so a single 401 is as visible as fifty 529s. The pool row above it carries the volume.
  • A dash with its reason when the deployment does not serve the record; a real zero said in words.
  • Polling at 60 s, paused in hidden tabs, like Accounts.

Open, for BGD

  • Should Accounts’ rows carry a status chip that links here, or is the tab enough?
  • Seven health reads a minute is fine here; on a pool of 30 accounts, should the broker serve the pool’s record in one read instead?
  • Should the timeline also offer 24 hours (drawn as a switch), or is 7 days the only range worth a page?

This is a proposal: nothing is built until BGD picks.
