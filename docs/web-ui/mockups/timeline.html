Missing, or could not find

  • No route lists events across tasks. There is no GET /v1/events; events live in each task's subcollection. A cross-agent event ledger (Variant B) needs a new route and a collection-group index, which is a Track A change and, for its shape, a request rather than a decision here.
  • /v1/attempts cannot filter by profile, state or workflow. Variant A applies those filters in the browser to the pages read, and the page says so.
  • A task that never had an attempt (QUEUED or READY all span, cancelled before it ran) is not on /v1/attempts. Workflow steps are filled from /v1/workflows/{id}; standalone ones are not drawn, and the empty state points at Agents › Waiting.
  • No figure counts the pages left: next_page_token says more exist, never how many. Shown as a dash with that reason.
  • I could not find a generation_fenced drawing anywhere today beyond the event row's gen N; the fence mark in these frames is new.

2–4Variants

Variant A

Swimlane per agent, grouped by workflow

Recommended

Idea

One lane per agent over wall-clock time, grouped under its workflow (standalone agents last). A bar is an attempt that held capacity; a hatched violet band is a park, which costs nothing; a thin grey line is waiting, which costs nothing; an amber cut ends a fenced generation; marks show cancel asked, cancelled, succeeded, failed and dead-lettered. Drag on the axis to zoom (same since/until and back chip as today's bucket zoom). Click a lane to see its attempts and newest events below; Open in Agents goes to the split view. Today's outcome ledger stays as the Outcomes page beside it, and a one-line outcome strip sits on Lanes.

Pros

  • Answers "what happened, and when" across agents and workflows in one picture; parks, fences and cancels each have a visual form
  • Built on routes that exist: /v1/attempts (since/until, paged) plus one events page per lane in view (order=desc), which also closes the S1 UI half
  • Invariant 1 is visible: only the solid bars held capacity
  • Keeps #185's ledger whole, as Outcomes

Risks

  • Profile, state and workflow filters run in the browser over the pages read; a busy tenant needs several pages for 24h
  • One events read per visible lane; lazy-read in view (as #377 does for cards) or the page is N+1
  • Tasks that never had an attempt are invisible unless a workflow read supplies them
  • At 30d or 90d bars are slivers; past 7d it should default to Outcomes or group lanes by profile
Variant B

One event ledger across agents

Idea

A single chronological ledger of events across every agent: time, agent, workflow, event mark, generation, detail, grouped by minute. Chips toggle event families; checkpoints and heartbeats are hidden by default because they are ~90% of rows. A row opens a side card with Open in Agents and "this agent only".

Pros

  • The 3am question ("what did the reconciler do at 03:12, to whom") has an exact answer
  • Fencing, cancels and parks are rows with their detail, not shapes to interpret
  • Works at any span, and on a phone it is just a list

Risks

  • Needs a route that does not exist: GET /v1/events over the events subcollections with a tenant filter and a time index (Track A, a request)
  • Volume: a two-hour run writes about 175 events (redesign-v2.md:816-820); a tenant's day is thousands
  • No sense of duration or overlap: you read time, you do not see it
Variant C

Workflow runs over time

Idea

Rows are runs: each workflow is one stacked bar (time per state) and expands into its steps with dependency elbows, reusing the workflow page's Timeline spans; standalone agents fold into one row. Open the workflow goes to its page.

Pros

  • Natural for workflow-heavy tenants: the question "where did the four hours go" per run
  • Reuses WorkflowViews.tsx spans and the per-workflow read

Risks

  • Standalone agents (most tasks today) become one folded row
  • /v1/workflows has no time filter; the overlap check is client-side over pages
  • Duplicates the workflow page's own Timeline mode, so two places draw the same thing

6Recommendation

Variant A, swimlanes, with Outcomes kept beside it

It is the only variant that shows overlap, waiting and parks as time, and it needs no new route: /v1/attempts gives the bars and one events page per visible lane gives the marks. B is the better tool for one exact question and is the lane's drill already (the Events card under a selected lane is B at one agent's scale); a tenant-wide B waits on a GET /v1/events request. C is the workflow page's Timeline repeated, and folds away the standalone agents most work is.

Small choices made in the frames

  • Timeline gets two pages in the panel, Lanes (new, the default at /timeline) and Outcomes (today's ledger, unchanged, at /timeline/outcomes).
  • Lanes uses today's span control and zoom: presets 24h to 90d, drag on the axis sets since/until, a back chip returns. Past 7d the page suggests Outcomes.
  • Filters: State, Profile, Workflow, Kind and Group by (workflow, profile, none). They run in the browser over the rows read, and the coverage line says so.
  • Bars are attempts, not tasks. A park is the hatched gap between two attempts; the expected resume past now is dotted.
  • A fenced generation is an amber bar with a dashed cut and a "gen N fenced" label; cancel_requested is an amber mark, not an end.
  • Selecting a lane shows its summary and newest events below; Open in Agents goes to /agents/recent/<id>/attempts (the split view). On a phone the lane opens a sheet.
  • A one-line outcome strip from /v1/outcomes sits above the lanes, with its own loading and error.
  • Example data throughout: task, workflow ids and times are invented, the tenant is "eng", the user is operator@example.com.
