# Board by state — Voice it › Projects (mock-up, `?voiceprojects=board`)

**The idea.** Projects is a board with one column per state: In progress, Waiting, Completed and Cancelled. Archived sits behind a toggle and appears as a fifth column. Each project is a card with its title, its next step (or what it came to), when it was last talked about, how many cards are open, a progress line, and a real Resume button. Pressing a card opens a side drawer with the project's brief (Goal, Decided, Open, Next) and its recent cards. It also has the actions that close a project: Mark completed and Cancel project. Resume opens Talk on a **catch-up**. A timeline of the project's sessions, picks, agent work and merges sits beside four panels: Done, Decided, Open and Plan. Each panel lights up while Voice it says it, and Skip catch-up ends the catch-up. In Talk, a strip of the projects open in this session sits under the heads-up edge. Each entry shows its state mark, progress and open-card count, and the current project is underlined. Switching projects by voice and starting a new one show up in that strip; an aside (a question outside any project) leaves it as it was.

## + What it gets right
+ **States can be told apart at a glance, by shape as well as colour** (rest, states). In progress is a half-filled circle, Waiting a pause, Completed a filled check, Cancelled a struck circle and Archived a box. Each column has a coloured top edge and each card a coloured left edge. Cancelled titles are struck through, and archived cards have one faint dashed outline. The meaning of each state is only in the (i) tooltip on its column head, which opens under the head and pushes the cards down rather than covering them (states).
+ **A state word appears only where something is asked of the owner.** The only word on a board card is the ask itself: "Your pick" on the newsletter flow and "Approve" on the rota planner. Every other state is said by the column (rest).
+ **The filter is the same control as the legend.** The bar's toggle chips each carry a count. Switching off the two live states leaves Completed and Cancelled with a "Show all" button to undo (filtered). Archived stays out of the way until asked for (rest compared with states).
+ **The brief is one press away and does not hide where the card sits.** The drawer covers the right of the board, while the picked card stays outlined on the left. Next is highlighted as the line to act on (brief).
+ **Closing a project is a deliberate step and can be undone.** One dialog closes the project as Completed or Cancelled, with an outcome line. An (i) explains that the brief, cards and agents are kept (mark).
+ **The catch-up tells the story and also shows where things stand now.** The timeline answers "how did we get here?". The four panels answer "what is done, what was decided, what is open, what next?". The panel being spoken is lit, and the dots show it is part 2 of 4 (catchup). The project's open cards wait below it.
+ **Many projects in one session are always visible** (two-open, switched, new-mid). The strip shows which project is current. Each other project in the strip carries its own progress, open-card count and any ask ("Your pick" on the newsletter flow). After a spoken switch, the cards area shows the new project's cards. A project started mid-session gets a small dot (its meaning in the title), and the earlier projects stay open. Nothing in the strip narrates what just happened: the captions say it.
+ **An aside does not take over the session** (aside). The answer to "what is the state of my running agents" appears as a dashed **Aside** panel. The strip still shows the bakery website as current and its cards stay below.
+ **Light and dark** both use only console tokens. **Phone 390**: the columns stack and the filter chips wrap to two rows (rest-390). On the catch-up, the ring is dropped (Live in the heads-up edge stands for it). The captions come right under the project strip, and the four panels come before the timeline, so the part being spoken is visible without scrolling (catchup-390).

## ! What it costs
! **Width.** Four columns fit at 1440, but with Archived shown there are five columns about 190 px wide, so titles and next steps wrap to two lines (states). Below about 1100 px the board needs fewer columns. On a phone it becomes a long stacked list, where a tab per state might scan better (rest-390).
! **Cards are shorter than table rows.** There is no room for both "last changed" and "last session". Only the last session is shown, and the date of the outcome (for example "Merged #214") is not (rest).
! **A column's tooltip moves the board.** It opens in the flow under the column head, so hovering an (i) pushes that column's cards down while it is open (states).
! **The drawer covers the right-hand columns** (Completed and Cancelled) while it is open (brief, mark). The project itself stays visible, but the other columns cannot be compared while reading a brief.
! **The catch-up is a big panel.** At 1440 the open cards fall below the fold under it (catchup). An owner who does not want the catch-up has to press Skip each time. A standing "skip catch-ups" preference would need its own decision.
! **The session strip is one more row on Talk** (two-open). With three or more open projects it wraps on narrower screens (new-mid). On a phone it takes two rows.
! **Waiting is the console's paused hue (purple), not amber.** That keeps amber free for warnings, but "Waiting" is not drawn in the colour a person might expect for it (states).
! **Not built, drawn from fixtures:** states that change, the brief, the catch-up beats, and a session holding several projects. The server side does not exist yet, and every value in these frames is invented.
! **Phone catch-up drops the ring** (catchup-390). The only sign that Voice it is speaking is the captions and Live; the timeline sits below the fold, above the sticky mic bar, which has a full-width backing.
